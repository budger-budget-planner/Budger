---
name: Alternative badger icon source
description: The visual source and constraints for the alternative home-screen badger icon
---

The alternative home-screen icon must reuse the established Budger badger face: the same cream forehead stripe, cheek shapes, muzzle, eyes, and ear treatment. It should use a dark graphite-grey gradient and a clean complete frame. Do not substitute a newly generated badger illustration with different ears or facial proportions.

**Why:** A visually similar but different badger breaks brand coherence between the app logo, splash screen, and icon selector.

**How to apply:** When revising the alternative icon, derive the face and ears from the existing logo artwork, preserve the established proportions, and verify the rendered icon visually before shipping.
