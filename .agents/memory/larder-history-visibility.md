---
name: Larder history visibility
description: Keep transaction history out of the Personal Larder and Great Larder card surfaces
---

Larder cards should focus on the current bucket balance and actions, not a history list. Stored entries and their balance calculations remain part of the data model; only the card-level history presentation is omitted.

**Why:** The history list made the savings surfaces feel cluttered and was explicitly removed while preserving Larder integrity.

**How to apply:** Do not re-add history rows, history toggles, or clear-history controls to the Larder card surfaces unless the user specifically requests them again. Keep pending Great Larder approvals separate.
